# Reel-watcher

A personal library for studying saved reels and developing original content from referenced techniques.

## Language

**Study**: An analysis of one reel containing source-backed observations and explicitly labeled interpretations.

**Observation**: Something visible, audible, or stated in a source, supported by a timestamp or source excerpt.

**Effectiveness hypothesis**: An interpretation of how a creative technique might help a reel. It does not establish that the technique caused engagement.

**Reference**: A reel selected as an example to inform an original content brief.

**Content brief**: A proposed original content direction containing three hook options, a beat outline, visual suggestions, and linked references.

**Creator profile**: Reusable preferences describing the intended audience, objectives, and content format, which can be overridden for a particular brief.

**Same subject**: A relationship between reels discussing similar topics.

**Similar creative approach**: A relationship between reels using related hooks, formats, or visual techniques, even when their topics differ.

**Creator agreement**: The extent to which different creators express a similar claim. Agreement is separate from evidence quality.

**Evidence quality**: The support presented for a claim, distinct from its popularity or repetition.
